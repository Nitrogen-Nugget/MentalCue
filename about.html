About Us



This website, MentalCue, is designed to provide users with a way to memorize lines for free! Believe it or not, this entire website was built by a single person (me), and GitHub Copilot... As an actor myself (in jr musicals...), I always wanted a free way to be able to memorize my lines easily. And now, I offer this to you, user. Enjoy your experience!
